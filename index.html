About

Marco Forletta is a Financial Stability Expert at the European Central Bank. He received his PhD in Banking and Finance at Bangor Business School and has master's degrees from the Toulouse School of Economics and Bocconi University. He works in the fields of financial economics, banking, and macroprudential policy.
